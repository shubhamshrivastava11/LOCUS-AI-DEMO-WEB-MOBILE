The problem

Your team changes decisions in Slack. Your AI agents still read last quarter’s docs.

Pricing, launch dates and architecture choices change every week, mostly in threads and meetings. The pages your agents read don’t change with them. People used to catch the stale answer before it reached a customer. Agents don’t.

Support and sales agents

A customer hears a price you retired.

The agent quotes the help center. The decision changed in #pricing on Monday.

Coding agents

A sprint rebuilds a design you rejected.

The agent proposes the cache the team ruled out in an ADR. Review catches it a week later.

Leaders and new hires

A settled call gets argued again.

Nobody can find who decided, when, or why, and the person who decided has moved teams.

How it works

Every answer shows its work.

Locus AI searches each connected tool with the asker’s access, answers only what a source backs, and links every sentence to where it came from. Select a sentence to trace it.
