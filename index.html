The problem

Your team changes decisions in Slack. Your AI agents still read last quarter’s docs.

At a growing software company, pricing, launch dates and architecture choices change every week, mostly in threads and meetings. The pages your support, sales and coding agents read don’t change with them. People used to catch the stale answer before it reached a customer. Agents don’t.

Support and sales agents

A customer hears a price you retired.

The agent quotes the help center. The decision changed in #pricing on Monday. The deal opens with a correction.

Coding agents

A sprint rebuilds a design you rejected.

The agent proposes the cache the team ruled out in an ADR. Review catches it a week later, after the work is done.

Leaders and new hires

A settled call gets argued again.

Nobody can find who decided, when, or why. The meeting happens twice, and the person who decided has moved teams.

Why the tools you already pay for miss it

Enterprise searchFinds every document that mentions pricing. Can’t tell you which decision is current.
Agent memoryRemembers one agent’s conversations. Not your organization’s decisions, owners or access rules.
Docs and wikisOnly as current as the last person who updated them, which is usually before the decision changed.
