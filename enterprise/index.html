Decision Guard

Agents check their plan against what your team decided before they act. An agent sends its plan to check_plan. Locus AI returns any conflicting decision with its owner and source. The agent changes course or asks the owner for an override, and the override is logged.

Proposed plan

Add a Redis cache in front of retrieval to cut latency.

Reversal impact

Decision change alerts

Running agents learn about a changed decision as soon as it is confirmed.

Agents subscribe to topics. When an owner confirms that a new decision replaces an old one, every subscribed agent gets the change and a short reason over MCP.

Ownerless decision handover

No decision is left without an owner when people move on.

When someone leaves or changes role, Locus AI finds their active decisions, suggests a new owner by team and scope, and queues the rest for review.
