Security review

Questions your security team will ask.

Straight answers, including where the answer is “not yet”.

Raw messages are encrypted and purged after 30 days. Only structured decisions persist: the summary, owner, date, source link and classification.

No. Every integration uses read-only OAuth scopes, and you choose exactly which channels, labels, spaces and projects it can read.

Access follows role, scope and classification. Confidential records need an explicit grant, and personal sources stay private to their owner, including from Admins.

SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning are part of the Enterprise plan. Contact sales for availability for your identity provider.

Locus AI uses Anthropic models through the commercial API: a small model to skip noise and a larger one to extract decisions. Content sent through the commercial API is not used to train models. Model providers are listed as sub-processors.

Today, access follows the roles, scopes and classification your admins set in Locus AI, and SCIM removes people who leave. Mirroring each tool’s own membership, and re-checking it at query time, is on the roadmap.

Each agent connects to the MCP server on behalf of a signed-in person and gets exactly that person’s access. There is no shared admin key for agents.

Decisions are returned to agents as quoted records with their source, never as instructions to follow. Screening captured content for injected instructions is on the roadmap.

We measure precision and recall on pilot data with each design partner, and share the numbers for your own sources during the pilot rather than quoting a general figure.

Not yet. Customer-managed keys and bring-your-own model provider come first; single-tenant deployment follows for enterprise customers.
